About

Planners integrated into Huawei MindSpore / D-Rec runtime; validated on Ascend-910 clusters up to 10K+ NPUs.Third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at Sorbonne Université and EURECOM, working at the intersection of AI Systems, Parallel Computing and Performance Engineering.
