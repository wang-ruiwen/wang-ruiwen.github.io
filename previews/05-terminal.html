# about
Third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at Sorbonne Université and EURECOM, working at the intersection of AI Systems, Parallel Computing and Performance Engineering.

I build profiling-free planners for hybrid-parallel LLM pretraining — validated on Huawei Ascend-910 clusters of up to 10K+ NPUs and benchmarked against Megatron-LM.
