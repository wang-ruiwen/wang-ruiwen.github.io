Background

I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science, jointly advised at Sorbonne Université and EURECOM by Prof. Raja Appuswamy and Prof. Chong Li, with industrial collaboration at Huawei Paris Research Center through the CIFRE programme. My research sits at the intersection of High-Performance Computing and Machine Learning Systems, and centres on one question: how do we systematically plan parallelism for foundation-scale training, without per-cluster profiling and without losing portability when the hardware or model changes?

My thesis, Systematic and Portable Optimisation of Hybrid Parallelism for Large-Scale Distributed Training, develops a profiling-free symbolic cost surface for the joint configuration of DP, TP, PP, VPP, SP, EP, and OP, and turns that surface into a sequence of automatic planners deployed on Huawei Ascend-910 clusters of up to 16,384 NPUs.

Research focus

  • Profiling-free cost modelling. Closed-form, hardware-parametric memory and communication models for transformer and MoE training under arbitrary recomputation policies.
  • Joint hybrid-parallel planning. A single optimisation across DP / TP / PP / VPP / SP / EP / OP, with integer-linear-programming kernels for pipeline-bubble and memory co-optimisation.
  • Re-planning & runtime fidelity. Dependency-keyed incremental re-planning across model revisions, plus structured constraint injection that closes the gap between cost-model predictions and production-framework runtime.

Systems

Five named planners built during my PhD. Each is a concrete instantiation of one of four production-readiness properties — portability without profiling, joint optimisation across all parallelism dimensions, incremental re-planning across model revisions, and runtime fidelity on production frameworks.

PRISM

SCA / HPCAsia 2026

Profiling-free symbolic memory-driven strategy planner. Predicts memory under arbitrary recomputation policies via closed-form per-component expressions; the communication side is modelled with collective-volume and topology-aware templates.

92–96 % memory-prediction accuracy (median error ≈ 7 %) across DeepSeek3 / Llama2-3 / Mistral / TextHawk / Llama-MoE on Ascend-910 up to 1,024 NPUs; up to 1.43× MFU speedup over Megatron-LM.

BMPipe

IEEE CLUSTER 2025

Bubble–memory co-optimisation strategy planner. Formalises pipeline idle time into a tri-category taxonomy — recomputation, imbalance, preparation bubbles — and solves a joint pipeline–memory ILP for very-large DNN training.

1.36× speedup over Megatron-Even on a 16,384-NPU Ascend-910 cluster; up to 1.70× under 1F1B-Interleave; ILP solves in < 200 ms at production scale.

H²O

Euro-Par 2025 · 🏆 Best Poster

Holistic hyper-parameter optimisation. A two-level optimiser searches the macro tuple (DP, TP, PP, OP, micro-batch) with a fast symbolic Delta cost model; the inner level refines layer-to-stage assignment and per-layer recomputation via ILP — the first joint solver across all macro degrees plus stage assignment.

+36.72 % speedup over the D-Rec baseline on 128-device clusters; 35.73 % MFU on DeepSeek-141B — without any accelerator-time profiling.

Concord

Under review · 2026

Dependency-keyed re-planning. Each closed-form cost evaluation carries a static dependency set κ and a runtime value signature σ; a formal equivalence result establishes that warm re-planning preserves the macro candidate set and the top-1 plan that a cold full search would return.

1.14–1.24× end-to-end speedup over an expert-tuned Megatron baseline on 256–4,096 NPU MoE workloads; 2.74× median planner wall-clock speedup over cache-disabled full search; 54 / 54 top-1 agreement, 0 / 162 stale-hit value mismatches.

ManuMatic

IFIP NPC 2025

Strategy injection for robust automatic hybrid parallelism. A structured interface lets users pin a small set of performance-critical operators while the planner derives a globally consistent strategy for the rest of the graph; built atop the D-Rec planner inside MindSpore and degenerates exactly to D-Rec when no constraints are provided.

Up to 2.24× over D-Rec on Mixtral-8×7B with expert parallelism (8 NPUs); 2.04× on Llama3-8B at 8 K context; 1.45× / 1.30× on Qwen2.5-72B at 32 K context (64 NPUs, combined with BMPipe).
