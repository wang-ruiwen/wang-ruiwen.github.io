About

I build AI infrastructure for large-scale LLM training, with a focus on hybrid parallelism, performance modelling and automatic strategy planning. I earned my PhD in Computer Science at Sorbonne Université and EURECOM in July 2026, through a CIFRE collaboration with Huawei Paris. My research turns compute, memory and communication cost models into practical tools for deciding how to partition and schedule a training run.

The planners developed through this work are integrated into MindSpore / MindFormers. Across these projects, validation spans dense, MoE and multimodal models, Ascend-910 clusters of up to 16,384 NPUs, and models above 1T parameters. The work also supported production training, including China Telecom’s DeepSeek-MoE deployment and TeleChat3-MoE. The systems, results and corresponding papers are collected below.

Research focus

How a model is partitioned and scheduled across accelerators determines much of the time, memory and cost of training. Finding that strategy still relies heavily on profiling and manual tuning for each model and cluster.

How do we systematically plan parallelism for foundation-scale training without per-cluster profiling, and without losing portability when the hardware or model changes?

I build analytical cost models and optimisation-based planners that predict a strategy’s behaviour before it runs. The systems below address memory prediction, pipeline scheduling, joint optimisation, incremental re-planning and expert guidance within this shared approach.

Concord

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

Can Hybrid-Parallel Planning Support Alternating Model–Strategy Design? Dependency-Keyed Per-Layer Primitives for Re-Planning.

Ruiwen Wang, Thibaut Tachon, Raja Appuswamy, Chong Li.

Incremental hybrid-parallel re-planning with dependency-keyed per-layer primitives, recomputing only what a model edit can invalidate.

~2.7× faster re-planning (median over audited model edits) with provably identical plan selection to a full search; validated on dense and MoE models at up to 4,096 NPUs.

PRISM

SCA / HPCAsia 2026

PRISM: Profiling-Free Symbolic Memory-Driven Strategy Planner for Large DNN Model Training.

Ruiwen Wang, Philippe Fang, Chong Li, Thibaut Tachon, Raja Appuswamy.

Osaka, Japan · Jan 26–29, 2026

Profiling-free hybrid-parallel strategy planning with symbolic memory models, recomputation-aware estimates and topology-aware communication costs.

92–96% memory-prediction accuracy (median error ≈ 7%) across DeepSeek3 / Llama2-3 / Mistral / TextHawk / Llama-MoE on Ascend-910 up to 1,024 NPUs; up to 1.43× MFU speedup over Megatron-LM.

H²O: Holistic Hyper-Parameter Optimization for Large-Scale Deep Neural Network Training.

Ruiwen Wang, Chong Li, Raja Appuswamy, Yujie Yuan.

ManuMatic

IFIP NPC 2025

ManuMatic: Strategy Injection for Robust Automatic Hybrid Parallelism in Distributed DNN Training.

Ruiwen Wang, Chong Li, Hongxing Wang, Raja Appuswamy, Yujie Yuan.

22nd IFIP NPC · Nha Trang, Vietnam · Nov 14–16, 2025

Strategy injection lets users pin performance-critical operators while a D-Rec-based planner derives a consistent hybrid-parallel strategy for the rest of the graph.

Up to 2.24× over D-Rec on Mixtral-8×7B with expert parallelism (8 NPUs); 2.04× on Llama3-8B at 8K context; 1.45× / 1.30× on Qwen2.5-72B at 32K context (64 NPUs, combined with BMPipe).
